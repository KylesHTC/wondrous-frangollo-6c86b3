OCC V129 — FRONT-SCREEN OPTIMISED COMPANY OFFERS & FUNDERS

This version targets the closed Samsung Galaxy Fold front screen (narrow/tall).

What changed:
- Company Offers and Our Funders now use responsive headings, images, cards and labels instead of one fixed-size screenshot each.
- Original OCC dark green/lime styling and image assets are retained where possible.
- Pages use vertical space and scroll naturally when needed rather than shrinking with excessive empty space.
- Partners navigation stays fixed at the bottom.
- .nomedia included so extraction should not add app assets to Samsung Gallery.

Other OCC pages are carried over from V128.
Partner messages and funder listings shown for demonstration are not live messaging or verified funding confirmations.

To deploy, extract the ZIP, then upload the inner OCC_V129_Gallery_Safe folder to your existing Netlify site (index.html must be at the deploy root). Keep a backup of V128.
